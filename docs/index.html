Your Cursor limit on the home screen

Cursor Limits shows your plan, how much of the weekly limit is left, when it resets, and your extra credits.

What you see

The pictures use example numbers: 46% left, reset on Fri, Nov 20 at 09:38, and $13.95 in extra credits. Your phone shows your own account.

On the cardWhat it is
Cursor · Super Your plan name. Super is only an example.
46% left How much of this week’s limit is still left.
Used 54.2% How much of this week’s limit is already used. The blue bar shows the same thing.
Reset The day and time when the weekly limit starts over, in your local time.
Extra credits Extra credits in dollars, separate from the weekly limit.

Three widgets

Touch and hold the home screen, then choose Widgets. Pick one of these.

Limits Card

The full card: plan, percent left, blue bar, reset time, and extra credits. You can drag it wider, up to the full width of the screen. On a screen with five columns, that is 5 cells wide and 2 cells tall.

Limits Wide

One row: percent left, extra credits, and the blue bar under them. It also drags out to the full width of the screen, one cell tall.

Limits

The small widget. Drag the edge to change the size. 1×1 shows the green number only. 2×1 shows the green and gold numbers side by side. 1×2 stacks them. Green is the percent left. Gold is the extra credits.

The ↻ button updates the numbers right away. The smallest 1×1 widget has no button. Tap it to open the app.

In the app

  • The screen is split. Mint on the left is the weekly limit and the day it resets. The dark half is extra credits. That date does not reset the credits.
  • Before you sign in, the bottom left says Sign in. Use Google, GitHub, or email. After that, tap the big percent to refresh now.
  • The moon or sun in the corner switches dark and light, including every widget on the home screen.
  • Tap an alert number. Minus stays on the left and plus on the right, and the digits do not move. One tap steps by 1. Holding speeds up. Percent runs from 1 to 100. Dollars run from $1 up to the extra credits you have now.
  • The first change turns that alert on. It is sent once when the number falls to the line, and it can be sent again after the number goes back up.
  • Swipe down on the screen to refresh. Done closes the refresh wheels. The system back button does the same. 00:00 means there is no timer. A timed refresh waits at least 15 minutes.

Put it on your phone

1
Tap Download APK and wait for the file cursor-limit.apk.
2
Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or from Downloads.
3
Open Cursor Limits and tap Sign in.
4
Touch and hold the home screen, open Widgets, and add Limits Card, Limits Wide, or Limits.

On a Samsung phone, updates can pause while the screen is off. In the app, tap Battery and choose Unrestricted.
