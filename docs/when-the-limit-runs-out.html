Cursor Limits

When the weekly Cursor limit runs out

Cursor Limits is a free Android widget for that weekly pool. It shows what is left, when the pool resets, and the prepaid extra credits. It is not made by Cursor.

On a current Cursor plan the included pool refreshes weekly. Unused percent does not carry over. When the pool reaches zero, the editor shows a notice. Cursor models are not slowed down. If prepaid credit remains, those models keep running and the cost comes out of that credit. Third-party models were already charged to that credit, not to the weekly pool.

Requests stop in two cases. The prepaid balance is also empty and automatic top-up is off. Or a monthly cap on extra usage is reached, in which case extra usage pauses even if credit is still there.

The weekly reset does not wipe prepaid credit. Credit stays until it is spent or it expires. Cursor publishes the billing rules at cursor.com/help/models-and-usage/usage-limits.
